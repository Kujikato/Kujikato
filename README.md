## Who I am

Security Operations Analyst specializing in **threat detection, incident response, and cloud security**, with a strong foundation in system administration and IT support. I work daily with modern security tooling and log sources to identify, triage, and contain threats across diverse environments, while continuously improving processes and playbooks within the SOC.

I hold the **eJPT** and **ICCA** certifications, which validate my skills in penetration testing, network security, and cloud-centric defensive strategies. I sharpen my expertise through hands-on labs and CTF-style platforms, focusing on SIEM monitoring, vulnerability assessment, and security hardening across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

My long-term goal is to move into a dedicated **Pentester / Red Team** role, leveraging my SecOps background to design realistic attack simulations and help organizations proactively find and fix their weakest points.
